ChromaMatrix Wiki

Purpose, workflow, calibration, and capacity.
🎯

Purpose

Store text or files as color dots that can be printed, scanned, and recovered offline.

↔️

How it works

Encode data, print the matrix, scan it, then decode it with calibration and error correction.

📏

What capacity means

Smaller dots and more colors store more data, but require better printing, lighting, and scanning.

🗜️

Payload compression

Brotli or GZIP compresses the payload before Reed-Solomon, reducing the matrix size while preserving automatic decompression during decode.

🛡️

ECC and binary safety

The default is 50% parity. ASCII-95 uses reversible base95 packing for arbitrary bytes; 256 colors maximize digital density but are less reliable in physical captures.

🏷️

Flatbed Scanner (300 DPI)

Letter: ~283 KB
A4: ~295 KB
Best with a flatbed scanner.

⚡

High-Res Flatbed (600 - 1200 DPI)

Pitch: 0.15 mm
Letter: ~787 KB
A4: ~820 KB
Reference capacity with 16 colors; 256 colors is a fragile digital ceiling.
