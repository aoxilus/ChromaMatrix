Sheet Capacity Report: Letter vs A4 Physical Limits

Physical limits and character density across optical capture tiers and Reed-Solomon redundancy
🏷️

Outdoor Sticker & UV Tag Print

Recommended Pitch: 0.90 mm (High contrast circular dots)
3" x 3" Sticker: ~4,200 chars (~4.2 KB / ~2 pages of emergency text)
Full Sheet: ~22,300 chars (22 KB / ~9 typed pages)
Uses 35% Reed-Solomon redundancy to survive outdoor weathering, scratches, and UV fading.

📄

Paper Cold Storage Backup

Recommended Pitch: 0.50 mm (Macro / Stand photo)
Letter Capacity: ~72,500 chars (71 KB / ~29 typed pages)
A4 Capacity: ~75,500 chars (74 KB / ~30 typed pages)
Ideal for archiving long documents, source code, recovery keys, and medical manuals.

🖨️

Flatbed Scanner (300 DPI)

Recommended Pitch: 0.25 mm (100 DPI optical dots)
Letter Capacity: ~290,000 chars (283 KB / ~116 typed pages)
A4 Capacity: ~302,000 chars (295 KB / ~121 typed pages)
A single sheet of paper stores an entire 300-page book or complex codebase!

⚡

High-Res Flatbed (600 - 1200 DPI)

Recommended Pitch: 0.15 mm (170 DPI optical dots)
Letter Capacity: ~806,000 chars (787 KB / ~322 typed pages)
A4 Capacity: ~840,000 chars (820 KB / ~336 typed pages)
Maximum theoretical single-page paper density with 8-bit 256-color palette.

Color Science & CIELAB Calibration Theory

Why ChromaMatrix uses perceptual CIELAB ΔE distance rather than Euclidean RGB.
📐

CIELAB Perceptual Uniformity

In standard RGB, a change of 20 units in green is perceived very differently than 20 units in blue. CIE L*a*b* models human visual perception where distance $\Delta E$ accurately reflects whether two color dots can be physically distinguished under variable ambient room lighting and shadowing.

🎯

Reserved Dynamic Range Markers

ChromaMatrix strictly reserves Pure Black (#000000) and Paper White (#FFFFFF) exclusively for the 4 corner alignment finders and perimeter timing tracks. No data dot or calibration swatch uses pure black or white, ensuring 100% dependable spatial registration.

🧪

On-Sheet Reference Swatches

Printed along the top and bottom borders of every matrix is a physical swatch of every active palette color. The vision decoder reads these swatches first to construct an adaptive color profile, automatically canceling out printer CMYK gamuts and warm/cool lighting tint shifts.

🛡️

Reed-Solomon $GF(2^8)$ Self-Healing

Using polynomial arithmetic over Galois Field $2^8$, ChromaMatrix automatically corrects scratched, folded, or stained physical paper cells. With 35% ECC redundancy, over a third of the physical sheet can be destroyed while recovering 100% of the original payload.
